## TADA Product Architecture: Three Layers of Command on One AI-Native Fabric


TADA organizes manufacturing decision-making into three horizons, each supported by purpose-built products running on a shared Industry Action Graph and Digital Duplicate.

## Strategic Command (10 Weeks to 10 Months)

Strategic Business Management (SBM) provides C-suite and business unit leaders with a unified view of profitability, risk, and agility across the portfolio. SBM creates a financial and operational model of your business—products, markets, channels, plants, suppliers, costs, capacity—that enables scenario planning, margin analysis, and strategic trade-off decisions.

## Core capabilities:

- P&L analysis by product, market, and channel with real-time variance tracking

- Scenario modeling for pricing, demand, cost, capacity, and tariff changes

- Working capital optimization through inventory and production planning

- Risk and resiliency assessment across supply, demand, and geopolitical factors

- Cross-functional collaboration workflows that tie every decision to financial impact

Outcome: Executives operate from a validated, continuously synchronized model that delivers strategic clarity and reduces time from insight to action.

## Operational Command (10 Days to 10 Weeks)

Six modular Systems of Action (SoAs) provide functional leaders with real-time workflows to plan, orchestrate, and optimize demand, supply, and resources:

## 1. Sourcing & Procurement SoA

Spend management, supplier collaboration, tariff and total cost management, contract optimization, and category strategies. Integrates sourcing data (e.g., Dun & Bradstreet), purchasing systems (e.g., Coupa), and general ledger (e.g., NetSuite) to provide SKU-level tariff risk, landed cost visibility, and proactive supplier performance tracking.

## 2. Supply-Chain & Logistics SoA

Network view of suppliers, plants, distribution centers, and carriers with lead times, capacities, constraints, and logistics orchestration. Monitors product quality, ownership, credit risk, and sustainability across domestic and multi-national footprints, enabling rapid response to disruptions and geopolitical shifts.

## 3. Production Planning SoA

Order board management, clean-to-build workflows, capacity and line balancing, material availability tracking, and short-sheet and constraint resolution. Synchronizes manufacturing ERP systems (e.g., SAP S/4HANA) to deliver adherence to production plans while managing outliers continuously.

## 4. Distribution & Finished Goods Inventory SoA

Finished goods inventory optimization, multi-echelon positioning, service level management, allocation planning, and distribution center orchestration. Integrates inventory management systems (e.g., KATANA) to balance working capital with customer service commitments.

## 5. Service & Support SoA

Aftermarket parts, service orders, asset uptime, field service operations, warranty exposure,


and lifecycle cost management. Extends the digital twin to installed base and customer sites for predictive maintenance and optimal parts positioning.

## 6. Strategic Accounts, Customer Sites & Fleets SoA

Customer-centric view across accounts, sites, and fleets: product availability, SLAs, installed base performance, usage analytics, and joint planning with key customers. Enables collaborative demand management and proactive issue resolution.

Outcome: Cross-functional teams work from the same real-time truth, with automated workflows that detect constraints, trigger alerts, and coordinate responses across procurement, production, logistics, and service.

## Tactical Command (1 to 10 Days)

Execution-layer tools cut across all six SoAs to support front-line supervisors, planners, and analysts:

- Real-time monitoring: Live dashboards of state, exceptions, and KPIs at product, part, and BOM level
